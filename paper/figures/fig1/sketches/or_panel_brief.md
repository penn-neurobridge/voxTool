# Figure 1, panel 1: implant illustration (brief)

**What it's for:** the first thing a reader sees. In one glance it should say
"electrodes are placed in a patient's brain during surgery", before the figure
moves on to the CT and VoxTool. See `or_panel_sketch.png` for the layout.

**Size:** a tall panel, 1 wide × 1.8 tall (e.g. 1000 × 1800 px or larger).

**Must show**
1. An **ECoG grid** lying on the exposed cortex: a flat sheet with a grid of
   contacts (4×4 to 8×8) and its cable leaving through the opening.
2. **Two or three sEEG depth leads** going in through small burr holes outside
   the opening, with a few contacts along each lead.
3. The **craniotomy opening**: skin and bone edge, cortex with folds.
4. A **sterile drape** around the head, enough to suggest the operating room.

**Style**
- Clean, flat illustration; no blood.
- Contacts in warm orange-red `#e4572e`, the colour used for electrodes in the
  rest of the figure. Otherwise muted skin, bone and greys.
- View from above; a slight tilt is fine.

**Avoid**
- Faces and real patient photos.
- Text or labels inside the art. Labels are added in the figure layout.

**Tools:** BioRender (free tier is fine for a draft). Before the final version,
Dan checks that the grid, leads and burr holes look right clinically.

**Hand back:** a PNG at full size (and the BioRender link if you can share it).
